Car Castle Chengannur

A premium car rental and wedding fleet website designed to turn visual interest into direct booking conversations.

Visit live website
IndustryRental and wedding cars
MarketKerala, India
StatusLive website

The challenge

Make the business easier to understand and contact.

The website needed to make the fleet feel distinctive, help visitors explore the offer quickly and move interested customers into a simple booking conversation.

Our approach

Structure first, then expression.

We used a fleet-first structure, confident typography, strong imagery and clear WhatsApp actions. Mobile layouts keep vehicle discovery and booking reachable without forcing users through a complicated form.

What we delivered

A complete public website experience.

  • Visual direction
  • Fleet showcase
  • Responsive design
  • WhatsApp booking
  • Content structure
  • Launch support

Published outcome

The launched website gives the business a focused digital showroom and a direct customer path from vehicle discovery to enquiry.
