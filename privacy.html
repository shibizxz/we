Your information, handled simply.

This website has no user accounts or analytics trackers. Information you choose to send directly is used only to discuss and deliver your project.

What this website collects

This website has no user accounts, no analytics trackers, and no first-party cookies. The project form does not submit information to our server. It prepares a brief on your device that you can copy, download, or choose to share through WhatsApp or email. We save your theme preference in local browser storage and your planner answers and selected package in session storage so they can carry into the contact page. Form contact details are not saved by this website. If browser storage is unavailable, the website still works without remembering these choices.

What you share when you contact us

When you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive the information you send — typically your name, contact details, business name, and project description. We use it only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss the project
  • Prepare recommendations, plans, and quotes
  • Deliver agreed project work
  • Provide support and maintenance you request

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your details
  • We do not add you to mailing lists you did not request
  • We do not use your materials for unrelated purposes
  • We remove information or project materials when reasonably requested, subject to legal or contractual obligations

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Conversations may happen through WhatsApp or email, which are governed by the privacy terms of those providers. Visiting client work linked from this website takes you to independent websites with their own policies.

Questions or requests

To ask about information you have shared, or to request a correction or deletion, message us on WhatsApp or email webappzzofficial@gmail.com.
