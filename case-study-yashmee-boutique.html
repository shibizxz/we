Yashmee Boutique — a storefront for floral hair accessories

An online boutique for handmade artificial flower strings, floral clips and bands, and bridal hair accessories. Shoppers can explore collections and place orders through WhatsApp.

Visit live store
IndustryFloral hair accessories
ProjectOnline boutique
OrderingWhatsApp

The challenge

Help shoppers find the right flowers for their occasion.

A jasmine string for a bun and a bridal veni set serve different needs. The storefront needs to help shoppers tell those options apart and picture how each piece can be worn.

The approach

Show the pieces in context.

The homepage pairs styled photography with close-up product imagery. Links to artificial flowers, wedding flowers, and style ideas give shoppers several ways into the catalog.

Storefront features

From browsing to ordering

  • Collections for flower strings, clips, bands, and wedding accessories
  • Style ideas for buns, braids, and bridal hairstyles
  • Catalog search, favourites, and a shopping bag
  • Ordering through WhatsApp

The catalog gives shoppers room to compare styles before starting a conversation with the boutique.
