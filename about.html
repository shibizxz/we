About the studio

Small studio. Full responsibility.

WEBAPPZZ TECHNOLOGIES is an independent digital studio in Cheruthuruthi, Kerala. We connect strategy, design, development, launch and visibility so clients have one accountable partner.

What we do

Digital products built around a real business goal.

Our work includes business websites, web applications, Flutter products, confidential in-house software, UI/UX systems, technical SEO, Google Business Profile support and digital marketing.

Projects are planned around the customer journey or internal workflow they need to improve. We keep communication direct, show work in reviewable stages and avoid adding features that do not support the agreed outcome.

Local presence, wider delivery

WEBAPPZZ is based in Cheruthuruthi, Kerala and works with businesses in India, the United Arab Emirates and other markets. Public work includes transport, automotive, industrial trading and engineering consultancy websites. Private UAE software engagements are described without exposing client names or internal systems.
